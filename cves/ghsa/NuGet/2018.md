- CWE: CWE-502, CWE-639
- CVSS_V3: `CVSS:3.0/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H`
- Affected NuGet/YamlDotNet: introduced:0 fixed:5.0.0
- Affected NuGet/YamlDotNet.Signed: introduced:0 fixed:5.0.0
