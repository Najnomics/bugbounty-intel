### GHSA-6528-wvf6-f6qg — Pycrypto generates weak key parameters
- Aliases: CVE-2018-6594 | Published: 2018-07-12 | Modified: 2024-10-18 | Severity: HIGH
- CWE: CWE-326
- CVSS_V3: `CVSS:3.0/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:N`
- CVSS_V4: `CVSS:4.0/AV:N/AC:L/AT:N/PR:N/UI:N/VC:H/VI:N/VA:N/SC:N/SI:N/SA:N`
