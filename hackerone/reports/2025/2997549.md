## Summary (team, by gus_tabares)

A vulnerability was identified at the WordPress site on `payapps.com`. This vulnerability allowed unauthenticated attackers to discern the email addresses of users who have published public posts. Autodesk has fixed the vulnerability and we thank @khoof reporting this issue.
