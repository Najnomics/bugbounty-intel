## Summary (team, by lindsey-uber)

@peuch found data exposure on Github - expired passwords and usernames for ESXi (a bare metal hypervisor). The researcher also found credentials to a SendGrid instance (uber_infra_devtools), which would have allowed them to log in to SendGrid and send email from any @uber.com address. However, this would not have given them the ability to see any sensitive Uber data. 

We enjoyed working with @peuch on this issue and look forward to their future submissions to our program.
