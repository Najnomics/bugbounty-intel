## Summary (researcher, by karimrahal)

I was able to turn an email verification link into a password reset link.

Proof Of Concept:
*https://www.binary.com/user/validate_link?step=account&verify_token=q4b4QVyLZD9daVpAdiXAIiAExC8DaGmqFPk8wNt9nTqAm7Pa&l=EN*
Remove "step=account" from the URL, then enter your email. After entering your email, you will be able to reset your password.
