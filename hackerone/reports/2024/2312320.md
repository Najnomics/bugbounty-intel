# H1 #2312320: Weak Email Verification: Newly Registered Users Can Bypass Email Verification Step and Log In

- URL: https://hackerone.com/reports/2312320
- Program: enjin (Enjin)
- Reporter: alpernae
- Weakness: Improper Authentication - Generic
- Severity: medium (score None; metrics null)
- Bounty: yes
- Asset: URL platform.enjin.io
- State: Closed/resolved | Submitted: 2024-01-11 | Disclosed: 2024-01-19
- CVE: -

## Summary (team, by bradez)

@alpernae was able to demonstrate that there was no check for email verification, allowing for a user to authenticate into the [Enjin Platform](https://platform.enjin.io) without ever having verified their email address. This has been addressed and it is now impossible for a session to be initiated without email verification being completed.

## Summary (researcher, by alpernae)

Response manipulation was discovered on the Enjin platform. It is a vulnerability that allows an attacker to manipulate the response to a specific request. Using the victim's email address, the attacker can register without the victim's permission and log in without verifying the email address.
