# H1 #1737943: IDOR may allow access to non-public photos

- URL: https://hackerone.com/reports/1737943
- Program: flickr (Flickr)
- Reporter: 0xcyborg
- Weakness: Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR)
- Severity: medium (score 5.9; metrics {"attack_vector": "network", "attack_complexity": "high", "privileges_required": "low", "user_interaction": "none", "scope": "unchanged", "confidentiality": "high", "integrity": "low", "availability": "none"})
- Bounty: yes
- Asset: WILDCARD *.flickr.com
- State: Closed/resolved | Submitted: 2022-10-17 | Disclosed: 2024-07-06
- CVE: -

## Summary (team, by pdokas)

By adding elsewhere-discovered photo IDs for non-public photos uploaded by others to a Flickr group via an upload batch run by the attacker the attacker could gain access via their membership is that group to the third party photos.

## Summary (researcher, by 0xcyborg)

This vulnerability allows an attacker to access any non-public photo just by knowing its ID. It also lets the attacker add any photo to a group without the owner's approval.
