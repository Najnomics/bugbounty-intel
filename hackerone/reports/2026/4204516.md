## Summary (team, by revive-mcp)

## Description

HackerOne community member 0xE2E reported that a manager, or a website user with permission to link banners to zones, could supply another agency's advertiser and campaign IDs to the zone-linking page and see the names and IDs of its campaigns and banners. The page checked access to the caller's zone but did not restrict the campaign and banner dropdowns to the agency that owned it. The disclosure was read-only; unauthorized linking was not demonstrated.

## Resolution

The zone-linking page now uses banner-selection filters that include the zone owner's agency when listing eligible campaigns and banners. Existing banner type and size restrictions are preserved.
