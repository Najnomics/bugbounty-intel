## Summary (researcher, by nikitastupin)

While testing `api.semrush.com` I've found that some inputs triggered MongoDB error on `/reports/v1/projects/:id/siteaudit/page/list` endpoint. Contents of `url` parameter was reflected in error message. Unfortunately WAF blocked my XSS attempts. Fuzzing a little bit further I was able to bypass WAF with `<object data=javascript:confirm(document.domain)>` payload.
