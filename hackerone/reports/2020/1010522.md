- Weakness: Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF)

- CVE: -

## Summary (team, by tt_sr)

A missing CSRF protection and open redirect vulnerability was reported in the TikTok Careers portal single sign on flow which is used by applicants to apply for TikTok positions. This flaw was quickly remediated and does not impact TikTok.com or mobile application.  We thank @lauritz for reporting this vulnerability to our team and for confirming the resolution.
