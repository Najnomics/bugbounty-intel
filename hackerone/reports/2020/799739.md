## Summary (team, by jmarshall)

In this report, the researcher identified a DOM-Based XSS vulnerability on `www.rockstargames.com/GTAOnline/features/freemode`. This type of attack can result in cookie theft, or enable CSRF and phishing attacks. With the researcher's help we were able to identify the cause of the vulnerability and resolve it.
