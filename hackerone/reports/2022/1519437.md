## Summary (team, by 42x42)

@is- reported to us a DLL Search-Order Hijacking vulnerability in `work-64-exe-v7.16.3-1.exe`. Reference: https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1574/001/
The underlying issue was found in an older version of `Squirrel.Windows`. Issue Reference: https://github.com/Squirrel/Squirrel.Windows/issues/1801

We've updated the utilised `Squirrel.Windows` to the latest available version `2.0.1` and published a newer version of 8x8 Work ➡️ `7.22 published on 08/26/22`: https://support.8x8.com/cloud-phone-service/voice/work-desktop/download-8x8-work-for-desktop
