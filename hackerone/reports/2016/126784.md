## Summary (team, by coinbase2)

Coinbase pushed out a feature whereby scanning a QR code inside the Coinbase Mobile app would complete payments under 0.1BTC without further confirmation.

Upon reviewing this report and further consideration of the possible security implications of this feature, it was reverted.
