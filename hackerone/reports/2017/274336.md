## Summary (team, by babak)

@ysx found a bitstripsforschools CNAME entry pointing to an unclaimed WordPress domain, which could be taken over by an external party.

The CNAME entry was for a product that is no longer active.

## Summary (researcher, by ysx)

An unclaimed WordPress domain mapping upgrade could be leveraged to assume the `blog.bitstripsforschools.com` subdomain and serve arbitrary content in the Bitstrips context. Thanks again to @babak for the swift communication and remediation.
