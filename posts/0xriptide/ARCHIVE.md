> A note to those building on chain in 2026:
>
> vibe coding + high code velocity + bull market pace + rapid TVL growth = bugs galore
>
> we see many teams building skyscrapers on sand with a "fix it later" mentality
>
> initially this works and they think they are geniuses
>
> ... but eventually
>
> someone will find the big, protocol wrecking, critical bug
>
> capital flight and brand destruction happens overnight
>
> entire savings lost, lives ruined financially, lawsuits filed, death threats received
>
> i've seen it happen time and time again and it will happen to YOU if this is your approach to building on the blockchain
>
> riptide's recommendations:
>
> 1) find a balance between securing the code and shipping fast. the security strategy should not be to push critical fixes like OTA updates for an XBOX game when any significant TVL is at risk.
>
> 2) invest in top tier human security talent. AI + expert SRs are a force multiplier. get a team that builds with a security first mindset from day 1.
>
> 3) properly incentivize responsible disclosure and treat your whitehats like kings. they are your last defense and will go out of their way to ensure your success if treated well
