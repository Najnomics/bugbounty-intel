2. Prioritise filenames with lifecycle words: `delete`, `old`, `backup`, `export`, `dump`, `cleanup`, `temp`, `test`, dated stamps (`2024-`, `_final`).
3. Fetch with no auth; if 200 and body parses as CSV/JSON with email/PII columns, it's a valid report. Grep headers for `email`, `phone`, `address`, `password`, `token`.
4. Report severity: unauth PII dump is typically High even if the program's triage starts it lower — attach a redacted record count as impact evidence.
