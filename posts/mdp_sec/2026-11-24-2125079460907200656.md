**Study (AI-assisted hunting):** DeepSeek V4.1 Flash vs two abliterated (refusal-removed) builds on 10 black-box labs × 40 attempts. Stock model had **0 refusals** already; abliteration made the model check the brief far less (110 → 31 rule checks per run), and instead it ran **2.8× more wordlists, 4.3× more request floods, 3.2× more failed commands**; solves 22 vs 17 (heavier) vs 22 (lighter), not significant (p=0.37). Full write-up saved: [blackboxarena-deepseek-v4-1-flash-abliteration-2026-11-24](../../writeups/mdp_sec/blackboxarena-deepseek-v4-1-flash-abliteration-2026-11-24.md).
**Harness takeaway:** do not buy "uncensored" models for hunting; abliteration removes scope/rules adherence (dangerous for RoE compliance — floods and brute force can get you banned) without improving solve rate. Keep a rules-of-engagement checker in the loop and count brute-force/flood actions as a metric.
