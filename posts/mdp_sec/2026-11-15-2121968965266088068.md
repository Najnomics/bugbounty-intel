## Analysis

**Bug class:** Business-logic / missing authorization on a "generate perk/entitlement link" endpoint → any free account mints paid perks, unlimited and non-expiring.
**Root cause (from the post):** Normal (legit) claim flow calls a generate endpoint that returns a perk link. Calling that endpoint directly as a free-tier user still minted a link: no tier check, no rate limit, links never expired.
**Harness check:**
1. Record a paid/eligible user's claim flow in a proxy; extract the generate/redeem endpoints.
2. Replay with a free-tier session; loop N times; check uniqueness and redemption of each link from a second free account.
3. Test expiry (redeem after 24h) and single-use enforcement.
**Takeaway:** Entitlement *issuance* endpoints are as sensitive as redemption endpoints — test both for tier checks and limits.
