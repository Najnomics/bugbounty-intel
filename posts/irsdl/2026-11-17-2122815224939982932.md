Trigger: `"A"*64 + "="` (65 bytes; on JDK 17 `"A"*128 + "="`); decoder must be JIT-compiled (warm-up traffic); only on x86-64 with avx512f/bw/vbmi + bmi2 (Ice Lake+, Zen 4+).
