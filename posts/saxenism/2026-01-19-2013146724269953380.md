> 2. The classic mistake:
> 
> `return strings.ToUpper(os.Getenv("SGX_MODE")) != "SW"`
> 
> Unset var --&gt; SW mode --&gt; verification skipped
> 
> Flip the logic:
> 
> `return os.Getenv("SGX_MODE") != "SW"`
> 
> Now unset = SAFE path. 
> Secure by default, always.

> 3. Runtime env vars WILL get misconfigured. Enforce HW mode at build time:
> ```
> SGX_MODE ?= HW  # default to HW
> 
> build-mainnet:
>     --build-arg SGX_MODE=HW  # explicit, no ambiguity
> ```
> If it's not in the Makefile AND your CI pipeline, it's still a ticking time bomb for you.

> 4. Always follow Defense in Depth.
> Never a single point of bypass:
> 
> Example:
> Go verification (bypassed in SW)
>              |
> Enclave verification (catches it)
>              |
> Consensus layer (2/3+ validators catch it)
> 
> One fails? Others catch it. That's the whole point.

>

> func VerifyRaCert()        // the real deal
