## Analysis

**Keyword tags (automatic, from the post text):** CSP / CSPT

**Bug class:** CSP bypass / credential theft via form-action + meta referrer (no JS).
**Root cause:** `form-action` does not inherit from `default-src`. With `default-src 'none'; form-action 'self'` and a reflected HTML sink, a two-stage self-posting form can (1) collect credentials, (2) re-inject `<meta name=referrer content=unsafe-url>` + `<meta http-equiv=refresh>` so Collaborator receives the credentials in the Referer.
**How found / tools:** PortSwigger Burp AT skill that (a) matches allow-list hosts against 350+ CSP gadgets from cspbypass.com, (b) checks form-action for self-posting form exfil.
**Harness takeaways:** When scanning CSP, always evaluate form-action independently; do not assume `default-src 'none'` stops credential theft.
