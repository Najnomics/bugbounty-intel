## Analysis

1. People who think these bugs aren't practically exploitable are wrong — he has exploited **php2shell, brotli2shell, java4shell and ruby2shell against live remote targets, not just labs** (nginx2shell not yet tried, suspected exploitable).
2. The distinction: exploitation is **not trivial or portable**. Unlike react2shell you cannot spray a generic PoC across vulnerable servers and expect shells — each needs significant target-specific work, and success on one target doesn't transfer.
**Harness takeaway:** model each "2shell" CVE as a *campaign* (recon → fingerprint exact build/config → per-target grooming) rather than a scan check.
