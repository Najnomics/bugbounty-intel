## Analysis

**Bug class:** ZK / cryptography assurance — formal verification of a proving system (Generalized Bulletproofs for Monero FCMP++).
**Summary:** zkSecurity reviewed the `generalized-bulletproofs` crate in `monero-oxide` (commit f3660043...), wrote a new knowledge-soundness proof, found no high/medium (4 low, 3 info), then published an independent Lean machine-checked proof that the interactive protocol is complete, zero-knowledge, and sound. Scope limits: Lean proof covers the interactive protocol in an abstract group only — NOT the Fiat-Shamir (non-interactive) version Monero ships, NOT the Rust impl, NOT the specific curves, NOT the FCMP++ circuits on top.
**Harness takeaways:** When assessing a "formally verified" ZK component, always pin down the verification scope: interactive vs Fiat-Shamir, abstract group vs concrete curve, protocol vs implementation vs circuits. Audit value concentrates in the gaps the proof does not cover (implementation, Fiat-Shamir transform, circuit layer).
