## Analysis

**Context:** reply to @PaulosYibelo's Upload2Shell (CVE-2026-125396, Ghostscript Type 1 font loader UAF → `-dSAFER` bypass → RCE through image/PDF processing; full research already saved under [writeups/PaulosYibelo](../../writeups/PaulosYibelo/)).
**Signal (@brutecat, Google VRP researcher):** Google runs Ghostscript **sandboxed in per-request, isolated gVisor VMs**, so the bug had no prod impact there.
**Harness takeaway:** when you reach a file-processing RCE (Ghostscript/ImageMagick/LibreOffice/ffmpeg), first characterise the sandbox (ephemeral VM? network egress? metadata service reachable? persistent FS?) — impact and payout depend on escaping or abusing the worker, not on the code-exec alone. Report the observed isolation boundary in the writeup.
