    > Immunefi Studio shows how each program behaves, flags duplicates and known issues before you submit, and works with your agents. When Review gives a report the green light, it goes out with no submission fee.
    >  
    > What AI changed for researchers
    > AI has changed the bug reporting system: researchers lost bounties to duplicates they couldn't see coming, picked programs blind learning how reports are treated only after finishing the work, and had agents find leads faster than they could check them, wasting time better spent hunting on sorting.
    > Studio is built around those three problems. It opens today, Nov 18, by invitation. It works on bug bounties and on audit competitions, starting with the Coinbase Audit Competition later today.
    > Why we built Studio
    > We built Studio because AI changed the work for the researchers who hunt and for the teams who read their reports. Hacks multiplied: DefiLlama recorded 290 in the first nine months of 2026, against 111 a year earlier. Accounting flaws went from 15 to 48 and oracle manipulation from 14 to 41, while key compromises went from 18 to 32. Over the same period, bug bounty submissions on Immunefi rose 136%, and unique valid findings doubled. Each new attack raises the odds of one large loss, while reading reports takes time teams don’t have. Guardrails slowed the flood of reports, but they hit honest researchers too.
    > "AI made reports cheap, but it didn't make good reports cheap,” said Mitchell Amador, Immunefi’s CEO. “Studio gives honest researchers the tools to spend their time on bugs that matter, and gives programs fewer, better reports. That is how we keep finding a bug worth more than exploiting it."
    >  
    > The hard part moved from finding a bug to judging it:
    > Is it already reported? Since April, one in four bug bounty duplicates on Immunefi repeated a confirmed bug. That's a researcher who was right, came second and earned no bounty.
    > Is this program worth it? How fast it responds and pays, which impacts it rewards, how crowded it is. Most researchers find out after the hours are spent.
    > Which leads deserve a report? Agents find leads faster than anyone can check them. Today that check is done by hand.
    > Until now, those answers sat in Immunefi's report history and in how each program handles reports. Studio Review checks your draft against that history, including known issues projects have shared with us privately, without exposing anyone's report.
    > Testing the upgrade
    > Studio was tested by a select group of researchers in closed beta. We compared the 24 beta researchers who ran at least as many Reviews as reports they submitted with advanced and top-ranking researchers without Studio between August 1 and October 22. They:
    > had a 2.5x higher valid rate;
    > were closed as duplicates 63% less often;
    > saw their valid rate rise 73% after they started using Review, while the other group's fell 17% over the same period.
    >  
    > One beta researcher earned a $50,000 bounty on a program they chose with Signals, and a second one won a $25,000 bounty. With the right tools, in their own words, researchers “started focusing on quality over quantity”.
    > These results come from a small group on bug bounties. They describe what happened, not a promise. Audit competition results will follow once competitions close.
    >  
    > The Studio tools
    > Studio follows the hunt in the order you work.
    > Pick the program (Signals). How each program behaves now: response and payout times, paid against closed, which impacts get paid, how mediations end, how crowded it is, over the last 7, 30 and 90 days
    > Learn the code (Instascope, Blueprint). The scope as code you can navigate, with out-of-scope and known-issue hints, and a map of where value can leave the protocol
    > Follow changes (Alerts and webhooks). Every scope and code change as it lands
    > Organize the work (Workspace). Cases, tasks and notes for each hunt
    > Check before submitting (Studio Review). A verdict on your draft in about 3 minutes, with what to fix
    > Plug in your agents (Studio MCP). All of the above, inside Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client
    > Get the green light with Studio Review
    > Studio Review checks your draft against Immunefi's report history, including private known issues, and gives a verdict. If it finds a duplicate, it shows where your draft overlaps, without exposing the original report. The verdict is ready, uncertain, high risk, duplicate or known issue, with what to fix.
    > When the verdict is ready on a bug bounty program, the report meets the criteria a triager would apply, so:
    > the report goes straight to the program, with no submission fee;
    > from next week, it will also skip the second automated triage step;
    > if the project closes it anyway, the closure doesn't count against your accuracy or your invalid streak.
    > If Review misses a duplicate or a private known issue it could see, you receive credits equal to the Review fee.
    > Plug your agents into Studio MCP
    > For researchers, agents are already part of how they work. Through the Studio MCP, your agents get:
    > Signals, scope, Instascope, Blueprint and Workspace cases, straight from the source, with no scraping;
    > alerts and webhooks the moment a scope or codebase changes;
    > every Review verdict and its feedback, so they learn which leads were duplicates, known issues or weak on impact. Sending drafts to Review straight from your agent is in testing.
    > Studio works with your agents, not instead of them.
    > Use Studio for Audit Competitions
    > A competition gives you a fixed window, often a few weeks, on code most researchers have never seen. Studio now sets the competition up for you:
    > the in-scope code at the competition commit, with known issues marked;
    > a map of how value moves through the protocol;
    > a workspace to manage your agents' findings;
    > your agents connected to all of it over MCP;
    > submitting from Studio, with the competition's triage feedback on each report, laid out in Studio and sent to your agent.
    > The fee, the Triaging Agent and the escalation path are the same as anywhere on Immunefi, and duplicates follow the competition's usual rules. Researchers can use Studio on the Coinbase Audit Competition, with its $250,000 reward pool. More details are coming soon.
    > How to use Studio
    > Studio is invite-only. It opens first to the Studio Ambassadors, the researchers who hold its first invites, including the alpha testers who helped build it. Each Ambassador can invite researchers they trust, with the first wave of invites open for three weeks. After that, Studio grows one invite at a time, as new members bring in the researchers they hunt with.
    > Once invited, activate Studio from your verified account at studio.immunefi.com.
    > Activate Studio with the Solo Hunter plan, and use the credits to hunt. You activate the Solo Hunter plan by buying at least 300 USDC of credits for the quarter, with no separate fee for the plan itself. Credits can be spent as you hunt, on Reviews, submissions, mediations and competition fees (the Coinbase audit competition included), at the same prices as anywhere on Immunefi. Credits stay valid for 12 months after you buy them.
    > FAQ
    > Does Studio Review decide whether my report gets paid? No. The program decides. Review tells you before you submit whether the report looks ready, uncertain, a duplicate or a known issue, and why.
    > Can I trust a duplicate verdict when I can't see the original report? The verdict shows where your draft overlaps the existing report, without exposing it. You can check the reasoning yourself.
    > Won't someone beat me to the submission while my report is in review? A Review takes about 3 minutes, far less than writing the report.
    > Do I pay twice, once for the Review and again to submit? Not when Review gives the green light: green reports skip the submission fee.
    > Duplicates of valid reports already receive credits equal to the fee. Is Review still worth it? You receive those credits once a report's outcome is known. Review helps before you submit. You get a verdict in about 3 minutes, a clear list of what to fix and no risk to your accuracy. A green verdict also skips the submission fee.
    > I already have my own agents and tools. Why switch? You don't have to. Studio plugs into your agents through the MCP. They get Immunefi's program data, alerts on every change and the verdict on every Review.
    > I hunt in bursts. Is the plan worth it? You activate the plan one quarter at a time. Credits you don't spend stay valid for 12 months after purchase. So they carry into later quarters, including one with a competition you plan to enter.
    > Do credits I receive for valid work activate the plan? No. Credits you receive for valid work, mediations or referrals pay for submissions, mediations and Reviews. Activating the plan always needs a credit purchase.
    > Which programs and tools are available today? Signals and Studio Review work across Immunefi bug bounty programs. Studio’s competition tools start with the Coinbase Audit Competition. Instascope supports smart-contract assets today.
