**AI auditing-agent methodology (Cyfrin's head of research):** Opus 5.5 High was *incorrectly rejecting* valid findings (loss of recall), not a prompt/methodology bug, and swapping in other Claude/GPT models didn't fix it. What worked: a dedicated **review agent that re-examines every logged rejection**, tuned via experiments to pick the best model for that job. Best price-performance reviewer: gpt-6.1-sol high (gpt-6.2-sol "unusable").
Lessons: (1) one mental frame per agent — competing frames in one agent degrade it; (2) cross-model review reduces error rates; (3) gpt-sol is a strong cheap cross-reviewer.
**Harness takeaway:** directly applicable to apex-core's disprove/validate gates — log every *rejected* candidate with its reason and run a separate cross-model "rejection auditor" over them to recover false negatives; keep each agent single-purpose (hunter, disprover, rejection-auditor) rather than one agent with multiple competing instructions.
