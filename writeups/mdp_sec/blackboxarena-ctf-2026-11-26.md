## How it works

1. **Start a lab**Sign in with GitHub (accounts must be at least 30 days old), open [Labs](/challenges), pick one and press **Start**. You get your own private instance (a copy only you can reach), at a random address on our lab domain `ctfbox.net`.
2. **Attack it over HTTPS**With whatever you like: a browser, scripts, scanners, your own AI agent.
3. **Capture the flags**A lab's four flags are earned in order: each one opens the way to the next. Paste each `BBCTF{...}` flag into that lab's flag box; your flags are yours alone and never change.

**Time:** an instance runs for 90 minutes. **Extend** adds 30 minutes whenever less than 30 minutes are left, up to 3 hours in total, and you have 6 instance-hours a day. One lab at a time.

**Rules in one line**Attack only your own instance, never the site or other players; no denial of service; never share flags or your lab address.
