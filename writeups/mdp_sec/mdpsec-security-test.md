I'm Marius du Preez. Since early 2026 I have been finding security problems full time through bug bounty programs, where companies pay independent researchers to find security holes before criminals do. I've also spent 9+ years running my own businesses, so I look at every problem I find through a business owner's eyes.
What I've seen worries me. My reports don't go straight to the business. They go to a triage team at the bug bounty platform first, which decides what gets passed on. That can take weeks, sometimes months. One of mine took five months to be resolved.
Serious problems also get lost. Someone reports a weakness, it's labelled low impact, and the business may decide it isn't worth fixing. Months later I find the same weakness and show it can be used to take over customer accounts. But my report is closed as a duplicate of the original "low impact" one, so the business never learns how serious it really is. The hole stays open.
And again and again, a serious problem I found had already been reported by someone else. Still not fixed. Still open to anyone who found it. With AI helping attackers find these problems faster than ever, leaving a hole open for months is a much bigger risk than it used to be.
So I'm doing something about it. I want to put what I find straight in front of the person who owns the business, with nobody in between, so it actually gets fixed.

I use AI to help with testing. It's part of how almost every business works now, and it's the only way to properly check a website and its apps and have your report back within 7 business days instead of a month.
But AI gets a lot wrong. Point an AI tool at your website and it will hand you a long list of alarming warnings. Most of them are false alarms or too minor to matter. From my own work, only about 1 in 10 things AI flags turn out to be real problems worth fixing.
Telling the difference is the part that takes skill and time. I check and prove every finding by hand before it goes in your report. You get the real problems, not a scary list that sends your team chasing things that were never there.
I test with my own accounts and steer clear of your customers' real information. Any personal details that turn up are blanked out on my own machines wherever possible before AI tools are used, and the agreement sets out exactly how data is handled.
