# XSS & CSRF for Beginners: Finding Easy Bug Bounties

## Find XSS & CSRF bugs that others miss. A beginner’s guide to consistent, easy bug bounties.

### Introduction

Have you ever looked at complex hacking write-ups and felt completely lost? You’re told to chase scary, advanced exploits, while the real, consistent money is often found in the basics.

What if you were guided through the two most common and most rewarding web vulnerabilities that are often just waiting to be found?

This isn’t about complex chains or zero-days. It’s about a clear, methodical approach to **Cross-Site Scripting (XSS)** and **Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF)** that forms the foundation of any successful bug bounty hunter’s journey. Let’s shift the mindset from “breaking in” to “understanding and improving” security, one bug at a time.

### The Hacker’s Methodology: A Simple Mindset

When starting with **web application security**, it helps to think like the application itself. How does it process information? Where does it store data? How does it communicate with users? This mindset shift is what separates successful bug hunters from those who get stuck.
