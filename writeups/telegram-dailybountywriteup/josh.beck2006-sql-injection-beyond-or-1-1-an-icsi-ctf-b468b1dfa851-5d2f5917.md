Lab Time: 30 minutes

In this activity, participants are challenged to go beyond simple ‘OR 1=1’ SQL injection to enumerate the databases, tables, columns, and records that exist across the MySQL installation on the target VM.

SQLMap will certainly work most of the time once an injection point is found, but in a live engagement the ability to manually work through the enumeration/discovery process outlined in this lab is an important skill. This lab sheet is designed to work both as an introductory activity and a point of reference!
