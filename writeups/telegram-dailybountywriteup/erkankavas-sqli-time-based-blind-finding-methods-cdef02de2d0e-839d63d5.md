# SQLi Time-Based Blind Finding Methods

Although I am not currently living in my home country, I decided to write this blog post about a SQL injection vulnerability I discovered in a locally significant newspaper in Turkey. Through this, I am also making my debut in the Medium ecosystem.

If you are involved in bug hunting within cybersecurity or want to actively apply what you’ve learned to make progress in this field, the most critical phase you need to focus on is reconnaissance.

Reconnaissance, or recon, is the discovery phase aimed at gathering as much information as possible. This phase is fundamental in all cybersecurity frameworks, such as NIST and MITRE, and is often placed at the very beginning (even if named differently).

The data obtained during this phase can help you stay proactive, whether you’re approaching it from an attack perspective or an incident response standpoint.

In fact, reconnaissance is just as crucial in real life and military contexts. Acting without understanding the details of a target area or conducting proper reconnaissance can have severe consequences. There’s no room for time-wasting. I recommend spending as much time as possible on this phase, approaching it strategically, and carefully selecting the tools you’ll use.
