*Ticko Paladin is our long-term community program for security researchers and testers who submit high-quality feedback. Members preview our two-week roadmap, join the monthly Ticko Paladin Roundtable, and compete for the Monthly Paladin Star reward.*

**Key Takeaways**

We launched the [Ticko Bug Bounty](https://docs.ticko.xyz/resources/ticko-bug-bounty) and the [Opening Session](https://tickoxyz.notion.site/Opening-Session-3e3705177e9581c9a215d17c91fd432d) feedback event on Beta Mainnet to catch vulnerabilities, bugs, and product friction early. One good report can fix an edge case in matching logic or clean up a step in the trading interface. Still, an exchange never stops evolving. New contracts, account features, and system upgrades ship every week. The people best prepared to test them are the contributors who already know how Ticko works.

We built Ticko Paladin so the relationship does not end when a ticket closes. It is a permanent community program with no end date. Paladins have a direct voice in our product roadmap, and standout contributors are recognized each month as the Monthly Paladin Star.

Getting started is straightforward. Join and verify in the [Ticko Discord](https://discord.gg/ticko), claim an Access Code in the [#access-code](https://discord.com/channels/1539120774584082452/1539210978510643231) channel, and use it to enter the platform. Send security vulnerability reports to [security@ticko.market](mailto:security@ticko.market). For reproducible bugs or product ideas, submit them through the Opening Session [feedback form](https://tally.so/r/D4LjZR) or the Feedback option under the Campaigns menu in the trading interface.

Our team reviews every submission. You do not need to win a bounty or an event reward to qualify as a Ticko Paladin. Valid reports sent after limited event slots are filled, useful findings outside a bounty tier, and clear product suggestions all count. What matters is the quality of your feedback. We will announce the first cohort in Discord three days after this post goes live and add new Paladins every week.

Every contributor who qualifies receives the Ticko Paladin role in Discord, an exclusive role badge, and access to the private [#ticko-paladin-hub](https://discord.com/channels/1539120774584082452/1569650266360250450) channel. This channel connects Paladins directly with the product and engineering team behind Ticko.

Inside [#ticko-paladin-hub](https://discord.com/channels/1539120774584082452/1569650266360250450), we share our development plans and progress for the coming two weeks before updates go live. Members can also propose new trading pairs and product features directly to the team. Once a month, we host the Ticko Paladin Roundtable to discuss product priorities, review recent updates, and announce the Monthly Paladin Star.

Each month, our product team selects one Paladin as the Monthly Paladin Star. When more than one contributor stands out, the team may select up to two Monthly Paladin Stars that month. Monthly Paladin Stars earn 1,000 USDT in rewards after Public Mainnet launches: 500 USDT plus a 500 USDT futures bonus. These rewards are on top of any Bug Bounty or Opening Session rewards.

We select the Monthly Paladin Star based on three factors: feedback quality, helpfulness in Discord, and trading activity on Mainnet. Trading on Mainnet is optional. We encourage Paladins to trade so they can test the live product firsthand. It is never required to hold the role or win the Monthly Paladin Star.

To keep the role active, Paladins submit at least one high-quality piece of feedback each month. Members also attend at least one of every two consecutive roundtables and stay active in Discord discussions. There are no message quotas or daily check-in rules.

Ticko Paladin is open now as a permanent part of our community. Join and verify in the [Ticko Discord](https://discord.gg/ticko), claim an Access Code in the [#access-code](https://discord.com/channels/1539120774584082452/1539210978510643231) channel, and explore [ticko.xyz](https://ticko.xyz/). Submit product ideas through the Feedback option under the Campaigns menu or the [feedback form](https://tally.so/r/D4LjZR). Send security reports to [security@ticko.market](mailto:security@ticko.market) under the rules on the [Bug Bounty page](https://docs.ticko.xyz/resources/ticko-bug-bounty).

Making every market accessible, fair, and safe for everyone remains our mission. The researchers and traders who test our product every week help us build a stronger exchange. Follow us on [X](https://x.com/tickoxyz) and visit our [docs](https://docs.ticko.xyz/) for the latest updates.
