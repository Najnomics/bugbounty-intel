In cybersecurity, the CIA Triad play Important role to build guides information security policies, System architecture, and risk management.

Core Pillars Breakdown :-

Confidentiality (Privacy & Access Control)

Integrity (Data Accuracy & Trustworthiness)

Availability (Reliability & Uptime)

CONFIDENTIALITY :-

DEFINITION:-

Confidentiality means protecting information from being accessed by unauthorized individuals. The goal is to ensure that sensitive data is only seen by people who have the right permissions.

Purpose:

To prevent data breaches, identity theft, and unauthorized sharing of private or classified information.

To ensure confidentiality, we use five basic methods:

i) ENCRYPTION:

Process of converting data (plain text) into a code (ciphertext) to prevent unauthorized access.

ii) ACCESS CONTROLS:

By setting up strong user permissions, you ensure that only authorized personnel can access certain types of data.

iii) DATA MASKING:

Data masking hides private details to keep them safe while still allowing the data to be used.

iv) PHYSICAL SECURITY MEASURES:

Ensure confidentiality for both physical types of data, such as paper records stored in a filing cabinet, and for digital information contained on servers and workstations.

INTEGRITY :-

DEFINITION:-

Guarantees that data remains accurate, complete, and untampered with. It ensures data cannot be modified or deleted by unauthorized individuals, typically maintained using hashing, digital signatures, and version controls.

AVAILABILITY :-

DEFINITION:-

Ensures that systems, applications, and data are reliably accessible to authorized users whenever needed. This is maintained through hardware maintenance, redundancy, data backups, and protection against DDoS attacks.
