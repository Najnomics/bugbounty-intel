> Philippines' Largest Crypto Exchange Coins ph Halts Transfers to and from Self-Custody Wallets
>
> Coins ph, the Philippines’ largest cryptocurrency exchange, announced that effective November 19, it no longer supports direct crypto deposits or withdrawals involving self-custody wallets. Users may only transfer assets to or from accounts at other regulated exchanges. Under the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Travel Rule requirements, all external transfers, regardless of amount, require counterparty details, wallet type, exchange platform, and transaction purpose. Incoming deposits will be credited only after the required information is verified.
