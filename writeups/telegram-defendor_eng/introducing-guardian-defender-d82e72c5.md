# Introducing: Guardian Defender

**Introducing Guardian Defender: our app for free post-audit protection, backed by bounty rewards for Guardian clients.**

At Guardian, we've always believed security is more than a rubber stamp. Web3 security is an ongoing, multi-faceted responsibility.

Today, we're doubling down on providing our clients with the best protection and security solution possible.

We're proud to introduce [Guardian Defender](https://defender.guardianaudits.com/): a new layer of protection for clients *after* their audit is complete, backed by bounty rewards, completely covered by Guardian.

## What's a Guardian Bounty Contest?

A Guardian Bounty Contest is a post-audit, fixed-duration contest where researchers can compete to find critical vulnerabilities in code already reviewed by Guardian. We’re putting real money on the line for any validated Critical Finding.

## What It Means for Protocols

For approved clients, Bounty Contests are an **extra shield** before going live:

- **You get free exposure to top-tier independent researchers.**
- You get formal post-audit validation and competitive review pressure.
- And if anything is missed, we pay the reward.

**It's the closest thing Web3 has to an insurance-backed audit.**

Every contest is managed, funded, and adjudicated by Guardian.

## What It Means for Security Researchers

For auditors looking to use our contest platform:

**Each contest is:**

- **Time-boxed**: 30 days per contest.
- **Exclusive**: Only hand-picked protocols that passed our full audit pipeline get listed.
- **Focused**: Scope is the audited smart contract codebase.
- **Payout-backed**

All you have to do is find something real. If your finding is:

- Unique
- Critical (loss of 20%+ of funds)

**you win the pot.**

You can read specific terms and conditions for participating in our bounty contests as a researcher [here](https://defender.guardianaudits.com/terms).
