NEW: Cyber's CYBER token appears to have been hit by a bridge exploit. Onchain data shows 5.21 million CYBER, worth about $1.1 million, was minted on Solana and released from Cyber's Ethereum bridge contract two minutes later.

Upbit and Bithumb have suspended CYBER deposits and withdrawals, citing a suspected security incident. The tokens were sold for ETH, and 575 ETH (~$968,000) now sits in a new wallet. Cyber has not commented.
