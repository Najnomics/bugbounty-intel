>
> Ledger has confirmed tampered hardware wallets in Berlin and is investigating two additional cases in Dubai, expanding the supply chain attack first discovered in Southeast Asia in October.

> The devices contain physical implants designed to steal users’ recovery phrases during wallet setup.
>
> Reported victims claim losses exceeding $320,000, 507,000 USDT and 1,300 SOL.
>
> Ledger says no software or chip vulnerabilities have been identified, and no confirmed compromised devices were purchased directly from its website.
>
> The company is offering X-ray inspections of suspected devices at its factory in France and warns that more compromised wallets may surface.
