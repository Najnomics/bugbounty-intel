# $25B Saved in DeFi Hacks: How Immunefi Makes Defense More Profitable Than Attack

The DeFi sector continues to suffer huge losses, with billions stolen in hacks in recent years. But a shift in cybersecurity strategy is transforming the landscape: **bug bounty programs that reward ethical hackers for uncovering vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them.**

## The $25B Defense Breakthrough

DeFi faced over **$1.4 billion in hacks during 2024**, with some of the largest breaches including massive bridge exploits. Despite this, **losses have dropped by 40% compared to 2023**, thanks in large part to enhanced bug bounty initiatives.

At the forefront is **Immunefi**, a leading Web3 bug bounty platform. They have paid out more than **$120 million to whitehat hackers** and estimate that these efforts have prevented over **$25 billion in potential losses across 500+ protocols**.

## The Wormhole Case: A $10M Payout That Saved Billions

Mitchell Amador, Immunefi’s CEO, highlights the landmark case of the **Wormhole bridge**, where a critical vulnerability could have frozen user funds worth billions. Through Immunefi’s bounty program, a whitehat disclosed the flaw in 2022, triggering the **largest-ever software bounty payout of $10 million** and preventing any user loss.

Traditional security audits often miss such post-deployment risks in dynamic DeFi systems. Bug bounty programs create ongoing, active defense aligned with how attackers operate - making defense **more profitable and timely** than attack.

## Challenges Unique to Web3 Security

Amador identifies major gaps in conventional audit methods:

- **Static audits:** One-time reviews fail to catch evolving, live contract vulnerabilities.
- **Underestimating incentives:** Attackers are financially motivated; bounties must outbid their potential gains.
- **Lack of blockchain expertise:** Many enterprise teams miss composability risks where vulnerabilities cascade across connected protocols.

Furthermore, attacks on **oracles** - which feed external data to smart contracts - are an under-addressed threat vector that can destabilize entire ecosystems.

## The Future of Security: Automation and Arbitration

Immunefi is developing automated defense tools, like their **“Security Swarm”**, which instantly detect and mitigate threats, narrowing attackers' advantage of lightning-fast blockchain transactions.

They also introduced a novel **on-chain arbitration system** for fast, legally binding dispute resolution in vulnerability disclosures - bypassing slow courts and improving global coordination.

## A New Ecosystem of Security Collaboration

Amador foresees a future where **security researchers become integrated partners** with projects rather than outsiders, driven by transparent incentives and decentralized governance.

This shift is attracting top talent from traditional tech into crypto, motivated by better financial rewards and the unique openness of Web3.
