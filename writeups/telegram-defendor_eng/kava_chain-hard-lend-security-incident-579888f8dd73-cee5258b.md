#### *Containment and Recovery Update*

*Incident date: 14 November 2026 | Report date: 16 November 2026*

On 14 November 2026, HARD Lend experienced a security incident that resulted in approximately 53.9 million KAVA being taken from the lending application. The identified exploit has been blocked, and measures preventing its repetition are in force.

The incident was confined to HARD Lend, a decentralized lending application on Kava Chain. Kava Chain’s underlying security was not compromised, and the exploit did not create new KAVA tokens. Individual user deposits were largely unaffected.

### Findings and Containment

The attacker exploited a lending-accounting vulnerability that inflated the value of BUSD collateral, enabling repeated borrowing and liquidation transactions to withdraw existing KAVA from the lending pool. Approximately 49.2 million KAVA was subsequently bridged to BNB Chain via LayerZero.

Protective measures now in effect prevent the attacker from repeating the identified exploit or continuing to drain funds through it.

### Investigation and Recovery

A portion of the attacker’s funds has already been frozen. Coordinated efforts involving ecosystem contributors, partners, relevant exchanges and other venues, and law enforcement are continuing to trace and freeze the remaining proceeds and pursue recovery of the stolen assets.

### Permanent Safeguards and Continued Recovery

The identified exploit has been blocked, and protective measures are in force. The planned retirement of HARD Lend’s legacy lending functionality is being accelerated. A governance proposal will be submitted to permanently disable new lending and borrowing through the required seven-day voting process. The protective measures remain in force throughout this process.

Remaining positions will be addressed through an orderly process, with further guidance provided to users. Efforts to freeze and recover the stolen funds will continue alongside this work.

These measures are specific to HARD Lend. Kava Chain and the broader Kava ecosystem remain active, with continued network operations, development, and activity across other applications and services.

Further updates will be provided as governance progresses and additional recovery milestones are confirmed.
