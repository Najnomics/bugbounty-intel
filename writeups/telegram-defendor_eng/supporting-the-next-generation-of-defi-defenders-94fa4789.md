# Supporting the Next Generation of DeFi Defenders

Introducing BlockThreat Community, a free sponsored subscription for students, aspiring security researchers, and public goods teams.

A heartfelt thank you to each and every one of the 115 contributors and to [**The DAO Security Fund**](https://thedao.fund/?ref=blockthreat.com) for supporting BlockThreat in the recent QF round.

At a time when everyone in the ecosystem is on edge from the never ending stream of hacks and incidents, the initiative offered not only much-needed financial support but also a major morale boost to the security community.

As promised, I will use all of the donated funds to launch **BlockThreat Community**, a free annual sponsored subscription for students, researchers early in their careers, solo defenders, and public goods teams who would benefit from high-quality blockchain threat intelligence but may not have the budget for a paid subscription.

BlockThreat Community will provide access to threat intelligence, coverage of the latest attack trends, monthly Top 10 DeFi attack vectors, defender tooling, post-mortems, research, and ecosystem security news to help the next generation of defenders learn faster and stay closer to the threats shaping DeFi security.

Thank you for reading, contributing, and helping make the ecosystem safer.
